<h2 id="rigging-specs">Installation & Rigging Guide</h2>

### 1. Harness Line Routing
Run your Dyneema lines through your harness's internal pulleys or low-friction rings, ensuring lines do not twist around reserve bridles or speedbag legstraps.

### 2. Brummel Hook / Quick-Connect Attachment
Attach the line ends to your paraglider's speed system risers via Brummel hooks or soft shackles.

### 3. Setting First-Step Length
In a simulator or harness hang:
1. Extend your legs fully to your harness footplate.
2. Adjust line length so the **first step** is reached comfortably with knees bent at approximately 90 degrees.
3. Verify that at rest (hands up, trimmed flight), the speed bar does not pull the glider's risers even when the footplate is pushed.

### 4. Full Bar Check
Push the bottom rung fully until the pulleys on your glider's risers meet ("pulleys touching"). Ensure your legs reach full lock just as maximum speed bar travel is achieved.
