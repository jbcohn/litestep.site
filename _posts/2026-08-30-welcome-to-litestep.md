Welcome to the new **LiteStep** homepage and engineering log!

For a while, `litestep.us` was forwarded to a living Google Doc when pilots and friends asked about custom speed bars or projects. While that got the job done, moving to a lightweight, static Markdown site hosted on GitHub Pages brings a whole new level of speed, clarity, and organization.

### What is LiteStep?

LiteStep represents the convergence of several passions:

1. **LiteStep Speed Bars**: High-performance, lightweight multi-stage paragliding speed systems crafted for pilots who want positive foot feel and zero hassle in flight. Check out the dedicated [Speed Bar Specs & Guide]({{ '/speedbar/' | relative_url }}).
2. **Aviation & DEM Tools**: Visualizing IGC flight tracks over digital elevation models, computing task statistics, and analyzing race lines.
3. **Generative Art & Topo Merch**: Turning mathematical symmetry, form constants, and California peak elevations into custom apparel and visual art.
4. **Engineering Notes**: Writing up Python workflows, music data management tools, and project retrospectives right here.

### How this site works

This entire site is written in Markdown and rendered natively by GitHub's Jekyll engine. Adding a new blog post is as simple as creating a `.md` file in `_posts/` and committing to GitHub.

Stay tuned for more flight logs, rigging guides, and software releases!
